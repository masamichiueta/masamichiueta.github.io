Before praise,
make it visible.

Daily study is invisible even to the child doing it. Measure the time and keep it, and the days in a row, the levels gained, and the badges collected become something they can see for themselves.

One tap to start

Start the timer with a single tap. Manual entry is there too, for logging a session after the fact.

  • A simple screen a child can use alone

Days in a row, and levels gained

One minute a day keeps the streak alive. Today, current streak, and this week are all on the home screen. Study time raises the level, and the next target is always shown.

One week, and thirteen

A stacked bar chart you can switch between weekly and monthly, plus a 13-week heatmap. Short swings and long consistency, side by side.

300 badges across 27 categories

Collected badges line up here, and "next badge" shows how far there is left to go.

  • 30 badges are free; Premium opens all 300

Print it and put it on the fridge

Monthly and yearly effort, laid out as an A4 PDF. For the fridge door, or for the teacher.

  • PDF export is a Premium feature

Also included

Reminders (this device only)

Set a reminder per child. The setting stays local to the device, and it skips itself on days already logged.

Parent mode and PIN

Protects editing and record management. The 4-digit PIN is stored on this device only and never travels.

iCloud sync

Syncs automatically within one Apple Account. For a family member on a different account, records move as a file.

Your child's records
never leave home.

Data lives on the device and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see it.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Your child's name and your email never reach the developer.

No developer server

Everything is stored in your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot read.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK. For an app aimed at children, this line is drawn strictly.

The PIN stays put

The 4-digit PIN is stored on this device only — not in iCloud, not on other devices.

Local notifications

Reminders are generated on the device. No push notification server is involved.

Take it with you

Records export to a file, which is also how they move to a device on a different Apple Account.
