What's in it

One rule, and a few different ways to play it

🔍

Find the one

A wall of emoji fills the screen and one of them is different. Nothing to learn, nothing to wait for.

⏳

One clock for the whole run

Time is a single pool shared by every stage, not a countdown that resets. Bank seconds on the easy stages, spend them on the brutal ones. That trade is the game.

🪜

Five tiers

Easy, Normal, Tricky, Brutal, Impossible. Stage 1 is 36 emoji. The last tier is a wall of 900, with exactly one of them wrong.

💬

Send one to a friend

Any puzzle you play can be passed on as a plain message. No link, no install, nothing needed at the other end — the text carries the whole puzzle. Paste one in and it becomes a puzzle you can play.

📅

One puzzle a day

Everyone gets the same daily challenge, and the daily leaderboard resets every night. Play it as many times as you like — only your best score stands.

🏆

Game Center

Leaderboards for best score, furthest stage, and the daily, plus eleven achievements. Signing in is optional; the whole game works without it.

📈

Records and statisticsPremium

How your scores move, how you do per tier, and the emoji that beat you most. Practice mode gives you any tier you like, no clock, as many walls as you want.

🎯

Difficulty that means something

Every emoji pair is actually rendered and measured for visual similarity. Pairs you have already seen stay out of your next run, and you can leave out pairs that differ only in colour.

How to play

This is about as much explaining as it needs

1

Look

Find the emoji in the wall that isn't like the rest.

2

Tap it

Clear a stage quickly for bonus points. A miss costs 5 seconds, a hint costs 8.

3

See how deep you get

The run ends when the clock does. You get three hints per run.
