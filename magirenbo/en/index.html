There is exactly one rule: find the emoji that isn't like the others and tap it. No tutorial, no stamina bar, no loot boxes. The only thing that grows is the number of emoji and how small the difference is.

One that's different

Somewhere in a screen full of emoji, one is different. Find it and tap it. That's the whole game.

  • No account. Plays fine offline.

One clock for the whole run

The timer never resets between stages. Seconds banked on the easy stages are what you spend on the hard ones — that trade is the heart of the game.

  • A miss costs 5 seconds, a hint costs 8
  • Three hints per run, and no more

From 36 to 900

Easy, Normal, Tricky, Brutal, Impossible. Stage 1 starts at 36 emoji; the last wall holds 900, with exactly one impostor.

Send a puzzle you just played

No link, no install on their end. The whole puzzle travels as plain text. Paste a message you received and it becomes a puzzle.

Also included

One puzzle a day

Everyone gets the exact same daily challenge. The leaderboard resets every day, you can retry as often as you like, and your best score is what stands.

Game Center

Three leaderboards — high score, stage reached, and daily — plus 11 achievements. Signing in is optional and nothing is locked behind it.

Difficulty with evidence

Thousands of emoji pairs were actually rendered and measured for visual closeness. Recent puzzles don't repeat, and colour-only pairs can be excluded.

How to play

This is probably all the explanation it needs.

1

Look

Find the one emoji in the grid that isn't like the rest.

2

Tap

Clearing faster earns bonus points. A miss costs 5 seconds, a hint costs 8.

3

See how deep you get

When the clock runs out, that's the run. Three hints, no more.

Your scores are
yours alone.

Scores live on your device and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see them.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. We never even ask for an email address.

No developer server

Everything is stored in your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot read.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, anywhere in the app.

Plays offline

The only network use is iCloud sync and Game Center — and the whole game works without either.

Game Center is optional

Sign out and nothing is sent. Every feature is still playable.

Sharing stays local

Puzzles go out through the standard iOS share sheet, and pasted text is parsed on your device.
