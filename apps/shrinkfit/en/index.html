Forget the
quality slider.

Instead of guessing what 70% quality will weigh, type the size you need. ShrinkFit adjusts the quality and keeps the best-looking result that fits. It only shrinks the image dimensions when lowering quality alone isn’t enough.

Type a size, nothing else

Enter KB or MB, or tap a preset: 500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB, 5 MB, 10 MB. Images already under the target are returned as they are.

  • Results land at 99% of your limit or less, keeping as much quality as possible
  • Define 1 MB as 1,000,000 or 1,048,576 bytes

Batch it, then compare

Pick many images at once, watch the progress, and cancel any time. Results show “original size → compressed size” at a glance.

  • Switch between the compressed and original image, with size, dimensions, and quality
  • Save to Photos, or share to Mail, Messages, and more

Remove location, convert to JPEG

Choose Remove Location (the default), Remove All, or Keep All. HEIC and PNG images convert to JPEG in the same step.

  • Photo orientation is applied to the pixels, so images never end up sideways
  • Transparent areas in PNGs become white

How it works

Three steps from too big to just right.

1

Choose images

Pick from your photo library or Files. Many at a time is fine.

2

Set a target size

Type KB or MB, or tap a preset. Choose the format and how metadata is handled.

3

Compress, save, share

Tap Compress. Check the result, then save it to Photos or share it.
