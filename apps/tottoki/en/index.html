What it does

Everything you need to capture, keep, and look back.

📷

One artwork, as many photos as you like

Photograph drawings, crafts, calligraphy, and clay. Keep a 3D piece from several angles in a single artwork.

✨

Scan flat pieces cleanly

Scan paper artwork with perspective correction and brighten dim photos. You can adjust or swap photos after saving, too.

🎂

Their age at the time

See your child's age on the day it was made, like "3 yr 2 mo." Pick a photo and the made-on date fills in from when it was taken.

🗂

A timeline by month, with search

Browse by month, filter by child, and search titles, memos, and types.

📚

Albums, plus automatic ones

Build your own albums and reorder them. Favorites, by-child, and by-school-year albums are made for you.

👪

Share with your partner

One library for two of you, both able to view and add. If you stop sharing, your data stays with you.

How it works

Three steps from artwork to memory.

1

Take or choose photos

Tap “+”, then choose photos or shoot and scan with the camera.

2

Add a title and a child

Fill in the title, memo, type, date, and child. You can edit them any time.

3

Look back and share

Browse the timeline and albums, or share the library with your partner.

Your photos
stay yours.

The developer never collects your data.

No developer servers

There is no server that collects your data. Your photos stay on your device and in your own iCloud.

No account to make

No sign-up or log-in. Sharing uses the iCloud of your own Apple account.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

Minimal photo access

Only the photos you pick are read. The app never browses your whole library.

Sharing is your call

Sharing starts only when your partner accepts your invitation, and you can stop any time.

Back up on your terms

Export every photo and its details as one zip, and keep it wherever you like.

See the Privacy Policy for details.
