The kids
get to decide.

Planning dinner never stops. Menurette turns just the deciding part into something the family looks forward to. The candidates are picked cleverly behind the scenes, and on the wheel every slice is the same size — where it stops is what you get, so it's fair to the kids too.

Flick it and watch it go

The wheel spins as hard as you flick it and slows to a stop, with a click and a tap for every slice it passes. The Spin! button works too.

  • The turn passes through the family in order
  • Someone else can take a turn just for today

Where it stops is dinner

A big emoji and the dish's name, with confetti and a fanfare. Tap Let's have this! and it goes into History and the turn moves on.

No thanks! cards keep the peace

Really don't fancy it? Play a No thanks! card and spin again. Everyone gets a few a week (one to start with, back every Monday), and the dish turned down stays off the wheel for the rest of the day.

Every dinner, remembered

History keeps the date, the dish and who spun. It also feeds the draw, so what you had recently comes up less often.

Side by side on iPad

Turn the iPad sideways and the wheel sits on the left, with whose turn it is and tonight's candidates on the right. Stand it on the table and gather round.

There's more

A smart, fair draw

Recent dinners, and dishes sharing yesterday's cuisine or main ingredient, come up less often; family favourites come up more. The weighting only decides what goes on the wheel — on the wheel, every slice is the same size.

Filters

Spin only Japanese, Western or Chinese dishes, only meat or fish, or only quick meals. Set it before you spin.

24 dishes to start with

Curry rice, hamburg steak, fried chicken, gyoza, hand-rolled sushi and more staples of Japanese home cooking. Add, edit or remove whatever you like.

Family favourites

Mark whose favourite each dish is. The more fans a dish has, the more likely it lands on the wheel.

Grown-up settings behind a sum

Editing dishes and family members, and changing settings, takes the answer to a simple addition — so little fingers can't delete anything by accident.

Quiet on silent

The sound effects stay quiet when your phone is on silent, and sounds and haptics can be turned off. Reduce Motion is supported too.

How it works

Setting up takes about a minute.

1

Add your family

A name, an avatar and a colour for everyone. Their order is the turn order.

2

Spin

Whoever's turn it is flicks the wheel. Where it stops is dinner.

3

Decide

Let's have this! makes it official. Or play a No thanks! card and spin again.

Your family stays
on your device.

Dishes, names and history are stored on the device and nowhere else. The developer can't see any of it.

No account

Nothing to sign up for or sign in to. We don't even ask for an email address.

No network

There is no networking code in the app at all. Everything works in airplane mode.

Not even iCloud

Your data stays on the device, and nothing syncs.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs, of any kind.

No permissions

No access to the camera, photos, location, notifications or anything else.

No links out

However the kids tap, the app never sends them anywhere else.
