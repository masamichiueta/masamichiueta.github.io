What's missing is
outside the photo.

When the ratio doesn't match, you normally crop the edges or add black bars. AspectFit extends the outside instead, using a background built from the photo you already have. The photo itself is never touched.

Pick where it's going

Wallpaper ratios (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch) and social ratios (story/reel, square, portrait, wide) are one tap away.

  • Choose by destination — no numbers to memorise

Six background styles

Gradient, multi-gradient, blur, stretch, blur-stretch, or solid colour. Every one is generated from your photo, so the seam disappears.

  • Double-tap the preview to re-centre it if you change your mind
