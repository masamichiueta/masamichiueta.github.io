Features

From quick entry during the round to looking back afterwards

⛳️

Quick entry

Record strokes, putts, and GIR per hole with steppers and a single tap. GIR is derived automatically from strokes and putts (with manual override).

👥

Track your groupPremium

Add yourself and any number of playing partners to one round — keep everyone's score in tournaments and rounds with friends.

🖼️

Scorecard & image sharing

A player-by-hole grid with OUT/IN subtotals and totals. Export the scorecard as a PNG and share it straight to your group chat — watermark-free with Premium.

📊

Stats & charts

Average score and the score trend are free. Premium unlocks average putts, GIR%, score distribution, best/worst, the all-time range, and per-player stats.

🗺️

Course management

Register par for 18 holes (or 9-hole courses). Set the location via search and check it on a map preview. The free plan holds 3 courses; Premium removes the limit.

🎒

My ClubsPremium

Record distance and loft per club and visualize gapping with a distance chart. Add a standard 14-club set in one tap.

🔟

Starting hole

Supports back-nine and shotgun starts. Score entry pages through holes in play order, with running totals to match.

🔒

iCloud sync & privacy

No account needed. Your data lives only on your device and in your own iCloud, syncing across devices. The developer can never access it.
