One-handed on the course.
In depth afterwards.

A few taps for strokes and putts is all it takes — the scorecard and your stats take care of themselves. TeeNote stays out of your way during the round so there is something worth reading when it ends.

A few taps per hole

Record strokes, putts, and GIR with steppers and a single tap. GIR is derived automatically from strokes and putts, so entry takes seconds (manual override available).

  • Supports back-nine and shotgun starts
  • Score entry pages through holes in play order, with running totals to match

A scorecard you can just send

A player-by-hole grid with OUT/IN subtotals and totals. Tap any cell to fix it on the spot.

  • Export the scorecard as a PNG and share it straight to your group chat
  • Premium exports without the watermark

See whether you are improving

Average score and the score trend are free. Premium unlocks average putts, GIR%, score distribution, best/worst, the all-time range, and per-player stats.

How far does that club actually go?

Record distance and loft per club, and visualize your gapping with a distance chart. Add a standard 14-club set in one tap.

  • My Clubs and the distance chart are Premium features

Also included

Track your group

Add yourself and any number of playing partners to one round — keep everyone's score in tournaments and rounds with friends. (Premium)

Course management

Register par for 18 holes (or 9-hole courses). Set the location via search and check it on a map preview. The free plan holds 3 courses; Premium removes the limit.

iCloud sync

No account needed. Your data lives only on your device and in your own iCloud, syncing across every device signed in to the same Apple Account.

How it works

Three steps from teeing off to looking back.

1

Create a round

Pick the course, your group, and the starting hole.

2

Enter hole by hole

Record strokes and putts with steppers — for everyone at once.

3

Look back

Check your progress with the scorecard and stats, and share as an image.

Your scores are
yours alone.

Your data lives on your device and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see it.

No account

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to. We never even ask for an email address.

No developer server

Everything is stored in your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot read.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, anywhere in the app.

Works without iCloud

Not signed in to iCloud? Every feature still works with on-device storage.

Location is optional

Asked for only if you set a course's location from where you are. Declining affects nothing else.

Sharing is your call

Scorecard images go through the standard iOS share sheet. You pick the destination.
